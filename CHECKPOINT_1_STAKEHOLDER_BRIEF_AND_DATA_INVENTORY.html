1. Project Context and Simulated Stakeholder Brief

Project Context: TradeSight Global Export Management is a Chennai-based international export logistics firm employing 45 professionals. The company manages 50 enterprise and mid-market B2B client accounts across 12 destination countries worldwide. The firm orchestrates shipments originating from Chennai Port (INMAA), Kattupalli Port (INKAT), and Ennore Port (INENR), supplying global markets with Engineering Goods, Pharmaceuticals, Textiles, Agricultural Commodities, and Specialty Chemicals.

Simulated Stakeholder Needs: Executive leadership (CEO, CFO, Head of Operations, Chief Compliance Officer) currently relies on disparate operational spreadsheets. They require an enterprise-grade Export Business Intelligence Platform with:

  • C-Suite BI Dashboard: Centralized visibility featuring 8+ KPIs with interactive drill-down by destination country, origin port, and product category.
  • Logistics Transit Tracking: End-to-end monitoring of transit lead times, comparing Sea Freight vs. Air Cargo against delivery SLAs.
  • Multi-Currency FX Analytics: Real-time variance tracking between booking exchange rates and payment settlement rates across 10 global currencies.
  • Client Profitability Rankings: Rigorous margin attribution to identify top-tier contributors vs. loss-making accounts.
  • Automated Compliance Exception Reporting: Weekly proactive alerts for customs inspection holds, documentation delays, and HS code discrepancies.
Scope Note: This brief defines the operational requirements, analytical boundaries, and data governance framework for the TradeSight Capstone track. Empirical findings presented herein reflect initial profiling across the official project data packs to establish rigorous baseline benchmarks.

2. Five Analytical Questions

# Analytical Question Analysis & Intended Output
1 What are the overall export sales results and category margins? Quantify total revenue (₹1.72B), shipment volume (1,250 orders), and average order value across 5 product sectors (Engineering, Textiles, Pharma, Agri, Chemicals); measure gross profit contribution by category.
2 How do Foreign Exchange (FX) fluctuations impact profitability across the 12 destination countries? Measure variance between booking FX rate and settlement FX rate across 10 currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SGD, JPY, AUD, CAD, SAR, ZAR); identify high-exposure currency corridors and net profit erosion.
3 Which B2B clients generate the highest profitability vs. margin erosion? Rank all 50 clients by net gross margin and total profit in INR; segment Top 10 high-value partners vs. Bottom 10 loss-making accounts; evaluate credit term compliance.
4 What is the baseline shipment transit performance and cause of delays? Calculate transit lead times by mode (Air Cargo vs. Sea Freight) and destination port; evaluate delivery variance against target SLAs; identify port-specific bottleneck drivers.
5 What are the primary compliance exception triggers and their cost impacts? Profile the 15.84% exception rate across root causes (Customs Holds, Documentation Deficits, HS Discrepancies); measure additional clearance fees and delay days to drive weekly automated alerting.

5. Initial Risks, Assumptions, and Follow-up

Operational Assumptions

  • Multi-Currency Pricing: International contracts are billed in foreign currency; conversion to INR occurs at settlement.
  • Transit SLAs: Baseline lead-time expectations are 3–7 days for Air Cargo and 12–45 days for maritime Sea Freight.
  • Incoterms: CIF / DDP terms apply; TradeSight accounts for ocean/air freight and export clearance overheads.

Key Operational Risks

  • FX Volatility Risk: High depreciation in JPY and ZAR creates net profit erosion (-₹1.23M overall).
  • Low On-Time Delivery (38.88%): Transit lags create operational friction and potential contractual penalties.
  • Compliance Bottlenecks (15.84%): Customs holds and paperwork delays drive incremental inspection expenses.
