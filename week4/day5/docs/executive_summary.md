# RETAIL SALES ANALYSIS
## Executive Summary

### 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes the Sample Superstore retail dataset to evaluate business performance, identify sales trends, and understand product and regional profitability. Using Python for data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), statistical analysis, and Microsoft Power BI for visualization, the project transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights that support better decision-making.

### 2. Methodology and Analysis

The analysis followed a structured data analytics workflow:

- Data Cleaning: Checked for missing values, duplicate records, and incorrect data types.
- Exploratory Data Analysis:** Examined sales, profit, orders, customers, product categories, sub-categories, regions, and monthly trends.
- Data Visualization:** Created charts to compare sales and profitability across different business dimensions.
- Statistical Analysis:** Applied Pearson correlation to examine the relationship between discounts and profit, and One-Way ANOVA to investigate profit differences across product categories.
- Dashboard Development:** Designed an interactive Microsoft Power BI dashboard to present key performance indicators and business trends.

### 3. Key Business Findings

The dataset contains 9,994 transaction records covering the period from 2014 to 2017.

| Performance Indicator | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | 2,297,200.86 |
| Total Profit | 286,397.02 |
| Total Orders | 5,009 |
| Total Customers | 793 |
| Overall Profit Margin | 12.47% |
| Highest Sales Category | Technology |
| Highest-Profit Region | West |

Technology generated the highest category sales, approximately $836,154.03. The West region recorded the highest regional profit at approximately $108,418.45. The analysis also identified loss-making sub-categories, including Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies. Higher discount levels were associated with lower average profitability in the analyzed dataset.

### 4. Actionable Business Recommendations

1. Optimize Discount Strategies

Review discount policies and evaluate their impact on profitability. Reduce excessive discounting on products where higher discounts are associated with lower profits.

2. Improve Product Portfolio Management

Prioritize profitable product categories and investigate loss-making sub-categories. Review pricing, procurement costs, and inventory allocation to improve product profitability.

3. Strengthen Regional Sales Performance

Study the successful sales strategies used in high-performing regions and apply suitable approaches in underperforming regions through targeted marketing, improved product availability, and customer engagement.

### 5. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how data analytics can help businesses understand retail performance and make informed decisions. By combining Python-based data processing, exploratory analysis, statistical techniques, and an interactive Power BI dashboard, the analysis identifies important sales and profitability patterns and provides practical recommendations for improving retail business performance.
