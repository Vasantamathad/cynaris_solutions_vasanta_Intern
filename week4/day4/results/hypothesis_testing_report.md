# Hypothesis Testing Results Report

## Objective

To use statistical tests to validate business claims and compare delivery partner performance.

## Dataset

- Total observations: 20
- Partner A observations: 10
- Partner B observations: 10
- Measurement: Delivery time in days

## Test 1: One-Sample T-Test

Business claim: Average delivery time is 3 days.

- Significance level: 0.05
- Sample mean: 3.16 days
- Result: Statistically significant difference detected

Conclusion: Reject the null hypothesis. The data provides evidence that the average delivery time differs from the claimed 3 days.

## Test 2: Two-Sample T-Test

Business question: Do the two delivery partners have different average delivery times?

- Partner A average: 2.91 days
- Partner B average: 3.79 days
- Result: Statistically significant difference detected

Conclusion: Reject the null hypothesis. Partner B has a higher average delivery time in this sample.

## Business Recommendation

The company should investigate Partner B's delivery process, identify operational bottlenecks, and monitor delivery performance.

## Limitations

The dataset is small and illustrative. Real business decisions should use larger, representative datasets and consider other factors such as delivery distance, order type, and location.
