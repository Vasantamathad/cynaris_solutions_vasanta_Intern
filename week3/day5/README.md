# Retail Sales Performance KPI Dashboard

## Project Overview
The Retail Sales Performance KPI Dashboard is a data analytics project designed to monitor and analyze retail business performance. It provides insights into revenue, profitability, customer behavior, regional sales, and inventory management through interactive visualizations.

## Objectives
- Monitor key business performance indicators (KPIs).
- Analyze revenue and profitability.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Identify sales trends and anomalies.
- Improve inventory and business decision-making.

## KPIs Analyzed
| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | Total sales generated by the business |
| Gross Profit | Revenue minus cost |
| Gross Profit Margin | Percentage of revenue retained as gross profit |
| Average Order Value | Average amount spent per order |
| Conversion Rate | Percentage of visitors who complete a purchase |
| Cart Abandonment Rate | Percentage of carts not converted into orders |
| Customer Acquisition Cost | Cost of acquiring new customers |
| Stock-out Rate | Percentage of products unavailable in stock |
| New Customers | Number of newly acquired customers |
| Revenue Growth | Percentage increase or decrease in revenue |

## Dashboard Features
- KPI cards for business performance.
- Revenue analysis by product category.
- Regional revenue comparison.
- Revenue trends over time.
- Customer acquisition and conversion insights.
- Cart abandonment analysis.
- Inventory and stock-out monitoring.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- Data Visualization
- KPI Analysis
- Business Intelligence

## Business Insights
- Revenue and profit provide an overview of financial performance.
- Category analysis identifies high-performing products.
- Regional analysis highlights sales differences between locations.
- Cart abandonment helps identify potential checkout issues.
- Inventory analysis helps reduce stock shortages.

## Recommendations
- Improve the checkout process to reduce cart abandonment.
- Focus on high-performing product categories.
- Improve marketing efficiency and conversion rates.
- Monitor inventory to reduce stock-outs.
- Analyze sales trends regularly to support decision-making.

## Conclusion
This dashboard transforms retail sales data into meaningful business insights. By tracking KPIs and analyzing trends, businesses can improve profitability, customer experience, inventory management, and overall operational efficiency.
