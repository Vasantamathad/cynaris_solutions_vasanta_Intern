Retail Sales KPI Narrative
Week 3 — Day 5: KPI Design | Retail Sales Performance Dashboard
Executive summary
The sample retail dataset reports total revenue of ₹45,800 and gross profit of ₹19,250, resulting in a gross profit margin of 42.03%. Average order value is ₹79.10 and the displayed conversion rate is 3.16. These metrics provide a baseline for reviewing sales performance and profitability.
Performance observations
The dashboard compares revenue across product categories and regions and shows revenue over time. Use these visuals to identify the categories and regions contributing most to sales and to observe changes across the January–March sample period. The dataset is small (12 rows), so conclusions should be treated as directional rather than statistically reliable.
Monitoring priorities
Revenue and gross profit should be reviewed alongside margin to avoid focusing on sales volume without profitability. Conversion rate and cart abandonment can help identify issues in the shopping journey. Average order value can support pricing, bundling, and cross-selling decisions. Stock-out rate and inventory levels should be monitored to reduce lost sales and excess stock. Customer acquisition cost and returning-customer rate help evaluate marketing efficiency and retention.
Data caveats
Targets in the KPI framework are proposed starting points, not industry benchmarks. Validate them with business context and historical performance. Confirm that Orders, Visitors, Carts, Customers, Inventory, and Out-of-stock counts are defined consistently. Some measures may be sensitive to the small sample size and the period covered.
Recommended actions
•Review category and regional revenue contribution weekly and investigate sharp changes.
•Track conversion and cart abandonment daily; test checkout improvements if abandonment is high.
•Review gross margin by product/category before discounting or increasing marketing spend.
•Check stock availability for high-revenue products and prioritize replenishment.
•Compare customer acquisition cost with repeat-customer performance before increasing campaign budgets.
